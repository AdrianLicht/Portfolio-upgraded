About Me

My Journey

My journey into web development began when I realized my passion for creating digital experiences that are both functional and visually appealing. Coming from a background in CAD design, I already had a strong foundation in visual thinking and problem-solving.

The transition from CAD to code was challenging but incredibly rewarding. I've dedicated countless hours to learning through online courses, tutorials, documentation, and building projects from scratch. This self-directed approach has taught me not just coding skills, but also how to research effectively, debug persistently, and continuously adapt to new technologies.

My Approach

My design background gives me a unique perspective on front-end development. I understand the importance of user experience, visual hierarchy, and attention to detail. I strive to create websites that are not only technically sound but also aesthetically pleasing and intuitive to use.

Through dedicated learning and hands-on projects, I've developed skills in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and modern frameworks. Each project in this portfolio represents a milestone in my learning journey and showcases my ability to transform ideas into responsive, user-friendly websites.

I believe in clean code, thoughtful design, and continuous improvement. When I'm not coding, you'll find me exploring new technologies, solving coding challenges, and expanding my skill set.

Beyond Coding

When I'm not coding, I enjoy working out and doing things with the ones i love. These activities help me maintain a balanced lifestyle and often inspire creative solutions to coding challenges.
