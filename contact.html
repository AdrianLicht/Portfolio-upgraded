Frequently Asked Questions

What types of projects do you work on?

I specialize in front-end development for websites and web applications. This includes portfolio sites, business websites, e-commerce platforms, and interactive web applications. I'm particularly interested in projects that focus on user experience and responsive design.

What is your typical process for new projects?

My process typically involves an initial consultation to understand your needs, followed by wireframing and design approval. Once the design is finalized, I move on to development, regular check-ins, testing, and finally deployment. I believe in clear communication throughout the entire process.

How do you handle project pricing?

I offer both hourly rates and project-based pricing depending on the scope and requirements. After our initial discussion, I'll provide a detailed quote that outlines all costs involved. I believe in transparent pricing with no hidden fees.

How long does it typically take to complete a project?

Project timelines vary based on complexity and scope. A simple portfolio site might take 2-3 weeks, while a more complex web application could take 1-2 months. I'll provide a detailed timeline during our initial planning phase and keep you updated on progress throughout the project.
